# New Hire Handbook

Welcome aboard! This handbook covers the basics for your first week.

## Equipment

All new hires receive a laptop voucher of 2,400 USD, redeemable within 30 days
of your start date. Accessories such as monitors and keyboards are ordered
separately through the IT portal.

## Time off

The company operates on an unlimited PTO policy with a minimum of 15 days
taken per year. Unused days do not roll over because there is no fixed cap.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week. Two days per week must be
spent in the office for teams located in a hub city.

## Payroll

Payroll runs on the last business day of each month. Salary reviews happen
once a year, every October.
