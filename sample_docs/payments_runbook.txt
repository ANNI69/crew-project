On-call Runbook — Payments Service

Primary on-call rotation: payments-primary. Secondary rotation: payments-backup.
Shifts swap every Wednesday at 10:00 UTC.

Escalation policy
If the p99 latency alert for /checkout exceeds 800ms for 5 minutes, page the
primary rotation immediately. If the alert persists for 15 minutes, escalate to
the payments-backup rotation and post in #payments-incident.

Rollback procedure
Deployments are released with Argo Rollouts. To roll back, run:
  argo rollouts undo payments-api -n production
then verify the canary in the payments-dashboard Grafana board.
